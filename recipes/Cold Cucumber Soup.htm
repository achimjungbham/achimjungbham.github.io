Title: Cold Cucumber Soup

Yield: 2 portions

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Cooking time: none

Category: Soup

Cuisine: Vegetarian

Source: Chefkoch.de

Ingredients

  • Soup
    • 1 large cucumber (about 400g)
    • 1 clove garlic, crushed
    • 500 g yogurt
    • 1 bunch dill
    • salt and pepper (to taste)
    • other herbs (optional)
  • Side
    • French bread
    • salted butter

Instructions

Peel the cucumber (potentially de-seed it if necessary) and cut it into cubes. Put all ingredients into a blender (possibly in two batches) and blend thoroughly. Put in a serving bowl and chill for at least one hour before serving.

Serve with French bread and salted butter.
