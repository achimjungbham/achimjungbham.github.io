Title: Lamb Keema

Yield: 2 servings

Cooking time: 50 minutes

Preparation time: 20 minutes

Category: Main

Cuisine: Indian

Source: Felicity Cloake

Ingredients

  • Spices 1
    • 1/2 tsp coriander seeds
    • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • Spices 2
    • 2 tbsp sunflower oil
    • 1/2 tsp coriander seeds
    • 1/4 tsp cumin seeds
    • 6 black pepper corns
    • 3 green cardamom pods
    • 1 small stick cinnamon
  • Aromatics
    • 1 red onion, chopped
    • 3 large cloves garlic, chopped or crushed
    • 1 small piece ginger, finely chopped
    • 1 green finger chilli, de-seeded and finely chopped
  • Meat
    • 70 g yoghurt
    • 350 g minced lamb (20% fat)
    • 1 green finger chilli, de-seeded and split lengthwise
    • 1 tsp salt
    • 1 tsp plain flour
  • Greens
    • 10 g mint leaves, chopped
    • 20 g coriander leaves, chopped
    • rind of 1 preserved lemon, finely chopped (optional)
    • 70 g frozen peas
  • Sides
    • 1 small cup basmati rice
    • 250 g tomatoes or some other salad

Instructions

Prepare onion, garlic, ginger, and chillies.

In a large pan, dry-roast the first set of spices until fragrant then take out and grind in a mortar to a powder. Set aside.

Heat the oil in the pan and fry the second set of spices until fragrant. Add the chopped onion and fry until soft, adding the remaining aromatics halfway through.

Add the yoghurt and bring to a boil, then add the meat, the salt, the ground spices, and the extra chilli. Fry until the meat juices start to separate out. Sprinkle with the flour and combine, then continue to fry for 20 minutes in the open pan.

Start a pot of rice for two people.

Prepare the herbs and the lemon rind. Add them to the pan together with the peas at the end of the 20 minutes and fry for 15 minutes more.

Serve with the rice and (tomato) salad.
